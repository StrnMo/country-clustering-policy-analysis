EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
This report presents the results of a comprehensive clustering analysis of 
168 countries based on 9 development indicators. The five-cluster solution 
identifies distinct socioeconomic profiles characterized by different 
combinations of health, demographic, income, and trade indicators.

A $1 billion development budget was allocated based on cluster membership, 
with higher priority given to less developed profiles.

KEY FINDINGS
------------
• Optimal Clusters: 5 clusters (K-Means algorithm, Silhouette Score: 0.2460)
• Algorithm Comparison: K-Means outperformed Agglomerative, Birch, and GMM
• Clear socioeconomic profiles identified from Low to Advanced Development
• Budget allocated based on needs: 35% to Low Development, 25% to Lower-Middle
